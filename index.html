Your ads × your flow

Your ads promise a method, a first sentence and a daily conversation. So we built three versions, one per promise.

The LingoLooper page runs 15 app ads in the Meta Ad Library, all with an Install now link to the App Store, in three promises: “The method diplomats and CIA use to learn a language: talk, get corrected, repeat” (6 ads), “Entiendes más de lo que puedes decir… Aquí hablas desde el primer minuto” (7, in Spanish, about English) and “Du verstehst mehr, als du sagen kannst” (2, in German). Every install still lands on the same listing and the same first launch.
